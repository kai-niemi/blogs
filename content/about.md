I'm Kai Niemi, a software engineer and architect based in Stockholm, Sweden, with over 20 years of experience designing
and building software systems.

My background spans software engineering, technical leadership, and enterprise architecture, with a particular focus on
distributed systems, databases, and high-performance transactional applications.

Over the years, I've worked on everything from application development to the architecture of large-scale,
geographically distributed platforms.

## About CloudNeutral

CloudNeutral is my personal technical blog about distributed systems, databases, and software architecture.

I write about engineering challenges, architectural decisions, and technical trade-offs, drawing on experience from both
hands-on development and designing complex software systems.

Topics include database internals, distributed architectures, performance engineering, and open-source projects.
